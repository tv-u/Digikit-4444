Security Information

We aim to maintain a reliable and responsible online utility platform. Security also depends on your browser, device, network and the third-party services you choose to use.

Protect sensitive information

Do not enter passwords, payment details, confidential business records or other sensitive information into a tool unless you have independently verified that the tool is appropriate for that information.

Browser-based processing

Some utilities may process information within your browser, while others may use external services or network requests. Do not assume that every tool processes all data locally. Review the behaviour of a specific tool before submitting sensitive information.

External websites and sponsored offers

Third-party websites may have different security and privacy practices. Check the destination and its policies before following an external link, downloading a file or submitting information.

Keep your device secure

Use an updated browser and operating system, install security updates, and avoid downloads from untrusted sources. Never share verification codes or passwords with an unknown website.

Reporting a concern

If you discover a suspected security issue, record the affected page and a description of the problem. Avoid sharing passwords, access tokens or other secrets in a public report.

Limitations

No website or internet connection can be guaranteed completely secure. This page describes general safety practices and is not a certification or guarantee of security.
